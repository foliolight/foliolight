# Trademark Policy

This policy is intentionally short.

1. The Apache-2.0 license covers the code. It does **not** grant rights to use the Foliolight name or logo (see Section 6 of the license).
2. You may use the name "Foliolight" to refer to the project and to unmodified releases you redistribute.
3. If you distribute a **modified version or fork**, please give it a different name. You may say it is "based on Foliolight."
4. Do not use the Foliolight name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement by the Foliolight project, its contributors, or any government agency or program.
5. Schema packs and tools that work with Foliolight may say "for Foliolight" or "compatible with Foliolight."
6. The Foliolight project itself does not use government names, seals, or program marks in its name, logo, or the design of its user interface, and refers to government programs only as neutral context.
7. Foliolight includes copies of schemas that government programs publish, and views for them. Where it does, it uses a program's name only to say what the schemas are and where they come from, never uses that program's logos, seals, or styling, and states that Foliolight is not affiliated with or endorsed by the program or any government agency. Program names and logos remain the property of their owners.

Questions: open a discussion or contact the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). The maintainer council holds the name on behalf of the community until it is transferred to a neutral steward.
